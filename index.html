FAQ

Quick answers

What is SelaamPeter?

An in-game agency. Businesses buy reviews from us, and we pay players in Rand to post them on partner review blogs.

Who can join?

Any player. You only need a username and the year your account was created.

How much does a review pay?

45 to 95 R, depending on the blog. That is multiplied by your account age: 1×, 1.5× or 2×.

When do I get paid?

The moment your review is posted, the Rand is added to your wallet.

Is this real money?

No. Rand is the in-game currency and has no real-world value. SelaamPeter is a fictional site, and nothing leaves your browser.
